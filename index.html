Load a sequence, choose which letter to send, and read every recipient before it goes. Each message stays personal, on brand, and in your hands.

Before you send. Confirm the sending domain has SPF, DKIM, and DMARC in place, and that the inbox has been warmed. Send from a dedicated outreach domain rather than your primary one, and post in small batches rather than all at once. Flagged rows are held back until their address is checked.

Direct send posts each letter to your own send endpoint, a small serverless function that holds your Resend key and calls Resend for you. The key never lives in this page. Paste the URL of your deployed function below, for example https://your-app.vercel.app/api/send, or /api/send if this page is hosted on the same project. Left blank, the desk opens drafts in your mail client instead.
